Weather Alert Display: Wiring

Elegoo ESP32 DevKit V1 → 1.8" TFT (blue board, 8-pin header). Confirmed working on breadboard 2026-10-09.

D4, D2 and D15 sit side by side. Double-check each one before soldering. CS one pin off (on D5) is what caused the white screen.

Pin map

VCC (yellow) goes to VIN on the ESP32's left side, bottom pin. It's not shown above.

Display power jumper (J1)

On the back of the display: J1 open (two separate pads) = 5V → VCC to VIN. If J1 is ever bridged with solder, move VCC to 3V3 instead.

Perfboard checklist

  • Female headers for the ESP32 and the display, so either part can be swapped
  • Display header placed near the ESP32's right side (7 of 8 wires come from there)
  • Each wire checked against the table before soldering, especially D4 / D2 / D15
  • Multimeter: no short between VIN, 3V3 and GND before first power-up
  • First power-up: colored test bars, then the weather screen
  • Colors right: red header, yellow temperature, blue barometer

Good to know

  • The blue LED on the ESP32 is tied to D2 (D/C). It lighting up is normal.
  • Solid white screen = the display never got its start-up signal. Check RST, CS and D/C first.
  • Colors look like a negative = the "invert colors" setting is wrong for that panel. This panel needs it off (already set in the program on the chip).
